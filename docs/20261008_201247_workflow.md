## Current publication view

1. The page opens with a plain-Japanese explanation of its purpose, the three available views, limits, a short glossary, and links to the Japanese README and interpretation policy.
2. Regional controls expose recognized cases, cleared cases, cleared persons, and the signed same-year recognition-clearance gap. The gap is never labeled as a strict unresolved count/rate.
3. The nationwide nationality selector exposes Japanese-inclusive cleared-person/case views and eight official foreign-nationality perspectives. A missing compatible Japanese numerator becomes a visible refusal.
4. Every row is shown in descending reference-ratio order; Japan uses a distinct residual-reference color, unavailable rows remain `未算出`, and the complete source-order table retains counts and ratios.
5. The nationwide nationality section also shows 2020–2024 for all 26 categories as a blue-to-orange heatmap with an exact numeric legend. Rows use average-linkage hierarchical clustering of row-standardized five-year patterns; unavailable-only rows remain last and years remain chronological. The line chart always compares the orange dashed Japanese residual reference with one blue selected category on a zero-based y-axis shared across all 26 categories. The exact annual table retains both series. Missing values stay `未算出`, and the three `その他` rows keep their source-region qualifiers.
6. The nationwide trend shows 2015–2025 all-foreign, visiting-foreign, and `all foreign − visiting foreign` residual shares of all criminal-code clearances, with a cleared-cases/persons control and a full count table. The residual is not labeled as resident foreigners; it includes every category excluded by the NPA visiting-foreign definition. These are clearance composition shares, not population rates. Historical years use S08/S09/S15; 2025 uses S21 Table 3-3-1 and the source change is stated in the UI.
7. A separate 2015–2025 section shows Japanese and all-foreign panels for the population denominator and per-1,000 reference ratio. It switches between cleared cases/persons, keeps the 2015 foreign numerator while refusing its unregistered denominator, and permanently states the residual, population-scope limitations, 2025 original Census basis, excluded unspecified status, and source break from previous population estimates.
8. The composition section shows all 26 categories across six offense groups as heatmap and 100% stacked views, with cleared-person/case and source/cluster controls.
9. Small denominator / sparse numerator warnings do not suppress rows. Zero-total composition rows show `構成比算出不能`, not a false 0%.
10. Values are descriptive public-data-derived reference ratios, clearance shares, or mechanical source differences, not official crime rates, causal claims, danger rankings, or statements about individual risk.

## Current phase: historical evidence

Historical official editions have been added without overwriting prior editions. The clearance-share trend covers 2015–2025 in v0.4.0; v0.5.0 also publishes 2015–2025 population-reference trends and 2025 regional context. Separately scoped 2025 nationality and visiting-foreigner offense views are implemented and being published as v0.6.0, with the original 2024 products retained. Structured Issue templates and an evidence-backed glossary beginning with `来日外国人` follow publication. Historical extensions must separate repeatable patterns from small-denominator volatility, report category/schema changes, and never manufacture a continuous series where definitions changed. NPA's monthly/year-end final-edition list reaches 2002, but this does not establish that the older editions contain the same tables as the annual detail publications. An optional e-Stat API path is later work: its FAQ restricts data retrieval to database-format statistics. Official-catalog discovery and scheduling must retain the last verified publication when a new edition fails validation.

Regional mappings have a separate pointer; the global mapping product is not
overwritten. The unrestricted mapping command currently stops on newer normalized
record types without reviewed dimension handling. Do not suppress that failure or
silently map unreviewed sources. Use the explicit reviewed regional subset above.

## Fixed publication rules

- Do not make a perfectly compatible numerator/denominator a project milestone; publish each available source pairing with its provenance and mismatches.
- Call project-derived values `公表統計由来の参考比率`, not official or exact crime rates.
- Keep raw numerators, raw denominators, formulas, dates, geography, population scope, and mismatch flags visible.
- Do not estimate unpublished `individual nationality × prefecture` crime numerators.
- Do not collapse NPA `中国`, `韓国・朝鮮`, source-region totals, or unresolved police geographies into a false common category.
- Keep NPA Table 13 geography as `police_reporting_area_unresolved` until an official definition establishes otherwise.

Detailed scope and results are maintained in [brief.md](./brief.md); permanent cautions are in [interpretation_note.md](./interpretation_note.md).
