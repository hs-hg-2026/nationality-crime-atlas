# Collection and Visualization of Crime Statistics and Resident Population by Prefecture and Nationality

Verify trustworthy data sources, regularly collect and update nationwide crime statistics and resident populations by nationality, prefecture, and region, and visualize population-based rates on GitHub.

日本語版: [README.ja.md](./README.ja.md)

## Background
Public data on resident populations and crime statistics are fragmented across publishers and may not share the same geographic, temporal, or nationality definitions. The detailed framing and cautions are maintained in [docs/brief.md](./docs/brief.md).

## Goals / Milestones
1. Audit primary sources and define comparable metrics.
2. Build a provenance-preserving collection and normalization pipeline.
3. Publish counts and only defensible population-normalized indicators with explicit limitations on GitHub.

## Approach
Define the numerator, denominator, geography, nationality category, and time period before data collection; then audit sources, prototype ingestion, validate rate calculations, build the visualization, and automate updates.

## Status
M1 (source availability audit) completed on 2026-08-30. `nationality/region × prefecture` population is verified, but a routine official `nationality × prefecture` crime numerator was not confirmed. The current gate is M0 publication rules: a count-only, provenance-first MVP is viable; a `nationality × prefecture` crime rate is not. See [docs/20260830_085603_source_availability_audit.md](./docs/20260830_085603_source_availability_audit.md) and [docs/brief.md](./docs/brief.md).
